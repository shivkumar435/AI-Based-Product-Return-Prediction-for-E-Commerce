## 🚀 Getting Started

### What is ReturnSense?

ReturnSense is an AI-powered e-commerce platform that:
- Helps you discover and buy quality products
- Uses AI to assess return risk for informed purchasing
- Provides seamless checkout and tracking
- Manages orders and returns efficiently
- Gives you personalized product recommendations

### System Requirements

- **Browser**: Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge (latest version)
- **Internet**: Stable connection (4G or WiFi)
- **Device**: Desktop, Tablet, or Mobile
- **JavaScript**: Must be enabled

### First Visit

1. Go to `https://returnsense.app`
2. Click "Register" to create account
3. Browse products
4. Add items to cart
5. Checkout and pay
6. Track your order

## 👤 Creating an Account

### Registration

1. Click **"Register"** on the navigation bar
2. Enter your details:
   - **Full Name**: Your name
   - **Email Address**: Valid email
   - **Password**: Min 8 characters, with uppercase and number

3. Click **"Create Account"**
4. Check your email for verification link
5. Click link to verify account
6. You're ready to shop!

1. Click **"Login"**

3. Click **"Sign In"**
4. You'll be logged in

**Forgot Password?**
1. Click "Forgot Password" on login page
2. Enter your email
3. Check email for reset link
4. Click link and create new password

**Can't Login?**
- Ensure email is correct
- Check password (case-sensitive)
- Verify account via email
- Try different browser
- Clear browser cache

## 🔄 Returns & Refunds

### Return Policy

- **Timeframe**: 30 days from delivery
- **Condition**: Unused and in original packaging
- **Process**: Online return request
- **Refund**: Within 5-7 business days

### Request Return

1. Go to **"My Orders"**
2. Find desired order
3. Click **"Request Return"**
4. Select reason:
   - Defective item
   - Not as described
   - Changed mind
   - Better option found
5. Add photos (optional but helpful)
6. Click **"Submit Return Request"**

### After Return Request

**Status Updates**
- Return Requested - Waiting for approval
- Return Approved - Bring to pickup point
- Return Rejected - Reason provided
- In Transit - Package picked up
- Returned - Received at warehouse
- Refunded - Money credited

**Return Shipping**
- Free return shipping
- Pickup point locations provided
- Print shipping label from order page
- Drop off at location

### Refunds

**Refund Status**
1. Go to order page
2. Find "Refund Status"
3. See current status

**Timeline**
- Approved: Within 24 hours
- In Transit: 3-5 days
- Received: 1-2 days
- Processed: 2-3 days
- **Total**: Usually 5-7 days

**Refund Method**
- Original payment method
- Original amount
- In same currency

### Cancel Order

**Can You Cancel?**
- ✅ Yes if status is "Pending" or "Confirmed"
- ❌ No if already "Shipped"

**How to Cancel**
1. Go to order
2. Click **"Cancel Order"**
3. Enter reason
4. Click **"Confirm"**
5. Refund processed immediately

## 🤖 AI Return Prediction

### How It Works

ReturnSense uses AI to predict return risk based on:
- Product category
- Price point
- Customer history
- Delivery time
- Similar orders

### Understanding Risk Levels

**🟢 Low Risk (0-30%)**
- Unlikely to be returned
- Safe purchase
- Average satisfaction

**🟡 Medium Risk (30-70%)**
- Moderate return probability
- Consider carefully
- Check reviews for concerns

**🔴 High Risk (70-100%)**
- High return likelihood
- Read reviews thoroughly
- Consider alternatives
- Check return policy

### Risk Factors

Each prediction shows factors:
- **Price**: Higher price = higher return?
- **Category**: Different categories have different return rates
- **Delivery Time**: Longer delivery = higher return risk
- **Customer": Your history with returns
- **Ratings**: Lower ratings indicate dissatisfaction

### What to Do

**Low Risk Items**
- Shop with confidence
- Good quality products
- Low return rate category

**Medium Risk Items**
- Read customer reviews
- Check specifications carefully
- Consider sizing/fit
- Review photos thoroughly

**High Risk Items**
- Read ALL reviews
- Watch product videos if available
- Check alternatives
- Consider if worth risk
- Read return policy

## ❓ FAQs

### Accounts & Login

**Q: How do I create an account?**
A: Click "Register" and fill in your details. Verify your email to complete signup.

**Q: Can I have multiple accounts?**
A: No, one account per email address.

**Q: How do I reset my password?**
A: Click "Forgot Password" on login. Follow email link to reset.

### Shopping

**Q: How do I search for products?**
A: Use search bar at top of page. Filter and sort results as needed.

**Q: Can I compare products?**
A: Yes! Check the compare checkbox on products (max 4). Click "Compare Now" button.

**Q: What's the return policy?**
A: 30-day returns for unused items in original condition. Free return shipping.

**Q: How long does delivery take?**
A: Typically 3-5 business days. Check order details for exact date.

### Payments

**Q: What payment methods are accepted?**
A: Credit Card, Debit Card, UPI, Net Banking, Cash on Delivery.

**Q: Is my payment secure?**
A: Yes! We use SSL encryption and PCI compliance.

**Q: Can I pay later?**
A: Cash on Delivery available. Check product page.

### Orders & Tracking

**Q: How do I track my order?**
A: Go to "My Orders" and click order number. See status and tracking info.

**Q: Can I cancel my order?**
A: Yes, if status is Pending. Go to order and click "Cancel Order".

**Q: How long for refund?**
A: Typically 5-7 days after return received.

### AI Predictions

**Q: Why does this item have high risk?**
A: Based on AI analysis of similar orders. Check factors listed.

**Q: Should I avoid high risk items?**
A: Not necessarily. Read reviews and check product quality. Risk is just a guide.

**Q: Is the AI always right?**
A: No. It's a prediction based on patterns. Your experience may differ.

### Technical Issues

**Q: App won't load?**
A: Clear browser cache, refresh page, try different browser, check internet.

**Q: Can't add to cart?**
A: Ensure you're logged in, item in stock, try refreshing page.

**Q: Payment not working?**
A: Check internet, try different method, contact support.
